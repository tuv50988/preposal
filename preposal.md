# Preproposal

## What idea(s) do you have for your final project?

Virtual wardrobe inspired by cher's digital closet in "Clueless". The program would allow the user to upload pictures of their clothing items, such as tops, bottoms, skirts, dresses, shoes, etc., to create a virtual version of their wardrobe. The user may have to input information about each item of clothing, such as the color, pattern, style, formality, etc. It would automatically generate compatible outfit combos based on this data and would also label clashing combos and explain why certain items may not coordinate.
